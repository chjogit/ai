When creating a soundproof space, the options mainly fall into three categories: soundproof booths, soundproof construction, and practice room rental. Each method shows clear differences in initial cost, soundproofing performance, and portability during moving, so the key is to choose one that fits your living situation and budget.

First, a soundproof booth is a method of installing an independent prefabricated structure inside a room, regardless of whether it is a private home, a lease, or a monthly rental. The initial cost ranges from about 2 million to 8 million won depending on performance and size, and the biggest advantage is that you can dismantle and move it when relocating, or sell it secondhand to recover the cost. It delivers performance well suited for vocals, MIDI work, and string practice, making it the most cost-effective option for renters or anyone planning to move within a few years. However, it has the drawbacks of having a cramped interior space, making it feel stuffy, and being sensitive to heat.

On the other hand, soundproofing construction is a large-scale project that installs specialized soundproofing and sound-absorbing materials throughout the walls, ceiling, and floor. For a 6-pyeong room, the cost is high—starting at a minimum of 10 million won and exceeding 20 million won—but it can provide the best soundproofing system to play drums or electronic music freely 24 hours a day while minimizing loss of space. Its ventilation and heating/cooling design is easy to implement, making the working environment very comfortable. However, since restoration to the original state is impossible after moving and the entire construction cost is lost, it is suitable for a professional musician who wants to live long-term in their own home and have a top-tier studio.

Finally, renting a practice room is the most flexible solution when it is difficult to raise a large upfront sum. Depending on the location and conditions, you can immediately use professional soundproofing facilities without a security deposit by paying only about 300,000 to 800,000 won per month. While it offers the convenience of clearly separating your home and workspace and allowing you to terminate the contract at any time, the accumulated monthly rent burden after more than one year of long-term use exceeds the cost of purchasing a soundproof booth, making it efficient for short-term projects, job seekers, or those with limited initial capital.

In conclusion, if you are a tenant and need a personal workspace, the most reasonable option is to purchase a soundproof booth used, as it may be recoverable. If you want a professional acoustic environment in your own home, soundproofing installation is recommended. If you want to start working immediately without spending a large sum of money, renting a practice room is a good choice.
